C6 Register Downloader - Bookmarklet Installer

This bookmarklet automates downloading C6 Register PDFs from the ESIC portal. No console needed!

Drag this button to your Bookmarks Bar:

C6 Downloader

Step 1: Show Bookmarks Bar

Chrome: Press Ctrl+Shift+B | Edge: Press Ctrl+Shift+B

Step 2: Drag the Button

  1. Drag the C6 Downloader button above into your bookmarks bar
  2. Release it when you see the bar highlight
  3. Done! The bookmark is saved permanently.

Step 3: Using It

  1. Login to gateway.esic.gov.in
  2. Click C6 Downloader in your bookmarks bar
  3. A panel appears on the page - upload your Excel file
  4. Click Start Full Auto Download
  5. If Chrome shows a popup warning, click Always allow
Excel format: Sheet1 - Column A - Header must be exactly Employer Code
